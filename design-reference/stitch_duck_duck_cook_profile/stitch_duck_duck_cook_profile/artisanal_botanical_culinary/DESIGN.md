## Brand & Style

The brand aesthetic merges premium farm-to-table craftsmanship with contemporary, approachable culinary hospitality. Geared toward discerning urban food lovers, families, and gourmet catering clients in an Indonesian culinary landscape, the visual identity radiates warmth, freshness, and unhurried artisanal care. It elevates humble, comforting poultry heritage into an epicurean experience.

The design movement combines **Warm Culinary Minimalism** with tactile botanical softness. Rather than stark industrial modernism or fast-casual neon brightness, the interface prioritizes airy negative space, warm organic textures, rounded architectural cards, and gentle herbal hues. Typography pairs an inviting, contemporary humanist headline with a crisp, neutral workhorse body to ensure effortless readability for detailed menus, ingredient provenance, and ordering flows.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep botanical herbs, pressed sage, and warm earthen tones. Deep Sage (`#5C7057`) acts as the anchor primary hue, conveying longevity, slow roasting, and organic kitchen mastery. It commands key calls-to-action, primary icons, and focused brand moments.

- **Primary (`#5C7057`)**: Brand identity anchor, primary interactive buttons, and emphasized pricing cues.
- **Secondary (`#89A482`)**: Fresh herbal medium green for badges, toggles, icon tiles, and interactive hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#ACC5A6`)**: Laurel green reserved for gentle card borders, progress indicators, and soft decorative accents.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**: Backgrounds default to `#FAF9F6` (warm off-white cream) paired with crisp card surfaces of pure `#FFFFFF`. Tinted surface layers use `#E8F5E9` and `#D1EDD3` for highlighting order status tags, dietary badges, or promotional pill headers.
- **Text & Contrast**: High-legibility deep espresso-charcoal (`#232D24`) provides authoritative typographic contrast, while `#3B4739` softens body copy and ingredient metadata.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances distinct editorial character with effortless digital readability. 

- **Headlines (Epilogue)**: A stylish, slightly geometric display face that captures warmth and craft. Used across dish titles, hero banners, and promotional storytelling to provide an appetizing culinary edge.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: An open, inviting geometric sans designed for clear micro-copy, nutritional notes, portion modifiers, and checkout steps in both English and Indonesian (e.g., *Pesan Sekarang*, *Pilihan Sambal*, *Detail Menu*).
- **Rhythm & Scaling**: Headline scale drops gracefully on smaller displays to avoid excessive line wrapping on Indonesian compound words and descriptive titles.

## Layout & Spacing

A responsive 12-column grid anchors desktop views (max-width: 1280px) transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column fluid layout on mobile devices.

- **Spatial Rhythm**: Generous whitespace is fundamental. Rather than packing food items densely like conventional delivery aggregators, listings breathe through `space-lg` and `space-xl` gaps, evoking the pacing of a tasting menu.
- **Breakpoints**: 
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (fluid vertical stacking, sticky bottom action bar, 16px lateral padding).
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (dual-column menu layouts, side filtering drawers).
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (multi-column presentation with fixed cart summaries and generous culinary photography columns).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Layering** combined with soft, warm-tinted ambient shadows, avoiding harsh grey drop-shadows:

- **Surface Levels**:
  - `Base`: Canvas wash in warm cream (`#FAF9F6`).
  - `Level 1 (Card/Resting)`: `#FFFFFF` surface bordered with subtle `1px solid rgba(172, 197, 166, 0.35)` and an ambient shadow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(92, 112, 87, 0.06)`).
  - `Level 2 (Hover/Active/Dropdown)`: Lifted state with `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(92, 112, 87, 0.12)`.
  - `Level 3 (Modal/Sticky Order Bar)`: Floated elevation using `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(35, 45, 36, 0.16)`.
- **Botanical Tinting**: Accent badges and highlighted order state cards use soft mint tints (`#E8F5E9` or `#D1EDD3`) to separate content planes without introducing drop-shadow noise.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an elegant, rounded geometry. Standard interactive elements and cards utilize `0.5rem` to `1rem` corner rounding, while signature menu item cards and presentation containers extend to `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` to `1.25rem`) to convey culinary softness and tactile charm. Small badges, chips, and quick quantity selectors take pill shapes to invite touch and rapid browsing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid Deep Sage (`#5C7057`) fill with pure white text, bold `label-lg` typography, and `rounded-xl` or full pill shape. Active/hover transitions smoothly into `#485944` with a subtle 2px scale effect.
- **Secondary Button**: Outlined in Soft Laurel Green (`#ACC5A6`) with Deep Charcoal text (`#232D24`) on a transparent or white background. On hover, fills with `#E8F5E9`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Text-only button in `#5C7057` with an underline transition for inline actions (e.g., *Lihat Detail Resep*).

### Cards (Culinary & Menu Cards)
- Built on a pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a gentle border (`#ACC5A6`, 35% opacity) and `rounded-2xl` radius.
- Imagery occupies the upper perimeter with an aspect ratio of 4:3 or 16:9, slightly inset or full-bleed to top corners. 
- Bottom container nests dish title (`Epilogue`, `title-md`), spice level/cut badge, portion descriptors, and pricing in bold primary green.

### Chips & Badges
- **Dietary & Flavor Badges**: High-contrast, friendly pills (e.g., *Rekomendasi*, *Pedas Gurih*, *Porsi Keluarga*). Background in `#D1EDD3` with `#232D24` text.
- **Filter Chips**: Inactive chips sit in `#FAF9F6` with a delicate border; active chips flip to `#5C7057` with white typography.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- **Text Inputs**: Crisp white field, `rounded-xl`, with a subtle Laurel Green border (`#ACC5A6`). Focused state rings with `#5C7057` (2px semi-translucent glow).
- **Portion Steppers (+ / -)**: Pill-shaped container in `#E8F5E9`, featuring Deep Sage icons and crisp digit displays for dish quantity selection.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Rounded checkboxes (4px radius) and concentric circular radio inputs colored in `#5C7057` when selected, with a reassuring spring micro-animation for accompaniment selections (e.g., sambal choices, rice varieties).

### Specialized Culinary Components
- **Spice & Flavor Meter**: Custom botanical icons (chili or herb leaves) filled sequentially in `#5C7057` to indicate roast spice depth.
- **Sticky Order Tray (Mobile)**: Floating bar at the viewport base (`Level 3` elevation), displaying total dish count, live price in IDR format (*Rp*), and a primary call-to-action button (*Lanjut ke Pembayaran*).
